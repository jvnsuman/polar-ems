Logistics Defense Context
In Antarctica, fuel cannot simply be ordered by tanker truck. Every single litre must be chartered on an expedition vessel (e.g. MV Vasiliy Golovnin) departing Cape Town, pumped across miles of sea ice, and hauled on tracked sleds across crevasse fields. Saving 41,600 Litres annually directly prevents hazardous sea transfers, eliminates 111+ tonnes of black carbon / CO2, and saves the national exchequer ₹1.39 Crore per year.
